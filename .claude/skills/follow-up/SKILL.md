# Debrief & Follow-up

From a meeting note (point me at one in `ops/` or `demo/meetings/`) or a pasted recap:

1. **Debrief (for you):** decisions made, commitments — yours and theirs — and any new risk or signal. Offer to append it to `ops/daily-log.md`.
2. **Follow-up (for them):** lead with the commitment you made or the decision they owe — not "just checking in". One clear next step with a date. Keep it short; a follow-up that needs scrolling doesn't get read.

Don't invent facts the note doesn't contain. Bring your own voice; this drafts the structure.

## After an event: the follow-up shape

Worked out from a follow-up the user actually sent after a conference. Use it as the default shape for event follow-ups, in this order:

1. **Greeting with their name linked** to their profile (LinkedIn), so they can see who wrote.
2. **One line of thanks that names the day you met.** No recap of the small talk.
3. **"As promised, …"**: say what you are sending and what it is for (usually their own review).
4. **"In one line:"** the single product sentence, with your company name linked.
5. **"From our conversation, I heard … places it might fit:"** a numbered list, two items at most, each in their words, with their company name linked.
6. **The material as an inline link** ("the Google Doc here"), and **one ask**: "I'd be glad to hear which is worth a closer look."
7. **Introduce the person who can go deeper**, by name, linked, and copied on the email, instead of offering to set up a call later.
8. **Play their decision process back to them**: "You mentioned you'll be reviewing … and will then decide …, so that's noted." It shows you listened and takes the pressure off. Only when they actually told you their process.
9. **"Warm regards,"** then your full signature.

Leave out: anecdotes from the room, self-deprecating hedges ("just as glad to hear not now"), internal context the reader doesn't need, and a second ask.

## Depth
- quick: the follow-up message only.
- standard: debrief notes + the message + a subject line.
- deep: + a second nudge for if they go quiet, with the date to send it.
