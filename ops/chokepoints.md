Whoever controls a chokepoint controls the flow through it, regardless of who owns the territory on either side. These are invisible while they work — nobody thinks about the single integration until it's deprecated, or the champion at the reference account until they change jobs — so the exposure only becomes legible once it's written down.

Two standing uses: the **risk read** (anything load-bearing with no named owner is the thing to fix), and a **news filter** (market noise that touches nothing on this list gets no attention).

**Re-rating rule.** A chokepoint carried unresolved for **3+ reviews** gets **re-rated, not dropped**. Repetition erodes a warning's perceived credibility, not its probability — the longer something sits here, the more attention it deserves, not less.

**Cross-check quarterly against the advantages you didn't earn** — the market tailwind, the partner referring for free, the inherited pricing position. Anything appearing on *both* lists is load-bearing **and** unowned, which is the top risk in any quarter.
